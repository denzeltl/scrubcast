Scrubcast brand

A halftone sound wave cut by a red playhead. Dots before the playhead are lit amber, dots after it are dim: what's been played, and what hasn't. The same wave is the app's scrub bar.

Logo

Mark, clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space of a quarter of the mark's width (dashed line) on every side. Below 40px use favicon.svg, which has bigger dots; it holds up at 16px. Don't recolour the playhead, add gradients or glows, or set the wordmark in another typeface.
