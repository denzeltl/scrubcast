# Scrubcast

## Direction
Scrubcast lets anyone ask a YouTube video a question and jump to the moment it's answered. The look is a broadcast monitor: a near-black deck, paper-white text, and amber reserved for signal. That means timecodes, actions, and whatever has already played. The halftone sound wave is the brand idea: it's the logo, the favicon and the scrub bar, so the mark and the main interaction are one shape.

It should feel precise and calm, never playful. No gradients, no glows except on the playhead and lit dots, no decorative motion.

## Colour
- **Deck (`#0a0b0a`) and panels (`#121412`)**: near-black, slightly green so it reads as equipment, not void.
- **Paper (`#efece2`)**: warm off-white text; muted (`#9aa093`) and faint (`#767d71`) for hierarchy. Faint still clears 4.5:1 on panels.
- **Amber (`#ffb020`)**: the only signal colour: timecodes, primary buttons, the played part of the wave. Text on amber is `#1a1200`, never white.
- **Pale amber (`#ffe3a3`)**: moments that answer the question, the brightest dots on the bar.
- **Playhead red (`#ff4d3d`)**: where you are now (the playhead, the "on air" dot). Never used for errors; errors use `#ff6a5a` with an icon and text.

## Typography
- **Inter Tight 800** for the wordmark and headings, tight tracking.
- **Inter** for reading: answers, overviews, transcript.
- **Geist Mono** for every number that ticks: timecodes, durations, scores, labels. Tabular figures so the readout doesn't jitter.

## Layout
- Sticky header: mark, wordmark, one-line promise, video picker.
- Step one: the source row (what you're searching, or paste a link). Step two: the question bar with suggestions.
- Two columns on desktop. Left: Answer → Passages, so the answer sits right under the question. Right: player deck → About this video → transcript, pinned, and it scrolls on its own when taller than the screen.
- Below `lg` one column, ordered: player → Answer → About → Passages → Transcript. Clicking a timestamp scrolls the player back into view.

## Motion
- **Scrub Lock** (signature): any timestamp click seeks; the playhead glides over 220ms with the out curve and the target column flashes amber (480ms fade). Reduced motion: the playhead jumps, the flash is 240ms.
- Buttons lift 1px on hover (pointer devices only) and press to 0.97; icon buttons press to 0.92 and nudge their icon toward the direction they act.
- Labels that swap (Play/Pause, Index video/Indexing…) crossfade in 140ms.
